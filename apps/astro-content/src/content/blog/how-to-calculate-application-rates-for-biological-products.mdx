## Why Biological Product Application Rates Are Different

Synthetic fertilizers are designed to supply nutrients directly to the plant.

Biological products, on the other hand, work by supporting the soil ecosystem. They introduce or stimulate beneficial microorganisms, improve nutrient cycling, enhance root development, and promote healthier soil biology.

Because every lawn and soil is different, biological products often have recommended application ranges rather than one fixed rate.

Factors that influence application rates include:

- Current soil health
- Organic matter levels
- Turf condition
- Maintenance goals
- Time of year
- Whether you're establishing or maintaining soil biology

This flexibility allows you to tailor applications to your lawn instead of treating every yard exactly the same.

## Common Application Mistakes

Even the best biological products won't deliver their full benefits if they're applied incorrectly.

Some of the most common mistakes include:

### Applying Too Little

Trying to stretch product farther than recommended can reduce effectiveness, especially when establishing healthy soil biology.

### Applying Too Much

More isn't always better. Applying beyond the recommended rate doesn't necessarily produce better results and may increase costs unnecessarily.

### Using the Wrong Unit of Measure

Mixing up square feet and acres—or estimating lawn size incorrectly—can lead to significant over- or under-application.

### Applying Too Infrequently

Biological programs are built on consistency. Following the recommended application schedule is just as important as applying the correct amount.
