## Why Most Lawn Programs Fail

A healthy lawn is about more than putting down the right fertilizer. One of the biggest factors in long-term turf health is what’s happening below the surface—specifically, the health and function of the soil.

Soil biology plays an important role in nutrient availability, root development, water management, and overall turf health. When the soil isn’t functioning well, adding more fertilizer may not solve the underlying problem. That’s why simply following a generic fertilizer schedule can fall short.

Two lawns can look similar but have very different needs based on factors such as soil conditions, turf type, growing environment, and previous lawn care practices. Applying the same products at the same rate and time to every lawn doesn’t account for those differences. Good lawn care starts with understanding the lawn first.
