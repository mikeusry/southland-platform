## Keep the words honest

- **Cleaning / maintenance** — organic load, flushing, routine care.
- **Sanitizing / disinfecting** — only where the EPA label supports the claim.
- **Equipment compatibility** — contact time and concentration that wreck seals are a fail.
- **Animal health** — this hub does not diagnose or treat disease.

If a sentence sounds like a veterinary claim, delete it and call support instead.
