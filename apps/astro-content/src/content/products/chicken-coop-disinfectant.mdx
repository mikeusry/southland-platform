## Clean Out, Then Disinfect

A coop clean-out has two steps. First, get the bedding, droppings, and loose debris out and scrape surfaces clean. Then disinfect what's left: roost bars, nest boxes, walls, floors, and feeders.

ZeroPoint handles the second step. It's an EPA-registered hospital disinfectant that kills 99.9% of bacteria when the surface stays visibly wet for 10 minutes.

## No Rinse, No Residue

Spray the surfaces, keep them wet for 10 minutes, and let them dry. There's nothing to rinse off and no chemical residue left behind. ZeroPoint is fragrance free and non-flammable, and after use it turns into a harmless dilute saline solution.
