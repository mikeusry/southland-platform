## A lawn care program, not a shelf of bottles

Most lawn products are sold one problem at a time. A bag for weeds, a bottle for
green-up, something else when the grass thins out in August. It works about as
well as taking a different vitamin every month and hoping.

A **Natural Lawn Bundle** is the opposite: a complete **DIY lawn care program**
built around soil biology, packaged as a single all-in-one kit with a schedule
attached. Four products, one per month, from early spring through the end of the
growing season.

## What's in every bundle

Every bundle contains the same four products. Your turf zone and lawn size change
the quantities and container sizes — never the roles.

- **Genesis** — humate soil conditioner. Brings out early-season colour and
  prepares the soil for summer. Turf treated with Genesis shows a 78% increase in
  green-up.
- **FertALive** — nutrient enhancer. Delivers nutrient-releasing microbiology and
  nitrogen in small, steady amounts rather than one heavy feed.
- **Omega** — soil activator. Biologically active carbon and organic acids, plus
  bacteria that promote nutrient uptake and stress resilience.
- **Revival** — liquid aerator. A microbial dethatcher that breaks thatch down
  organically instead of mechanical aeration.

## Nine bundles, because one size does not fit a lawn

There are nine bundles: three turf zones by three lawn sizes. Cool-season zones
apply May through September, transition zones April through September, and
warm-season zones March through August. Small covers up to about 5,000 square
feet, medium 5,000 to 10,000, and large anything above that.
